# Privacy policy

AutoScope does not collect, transmit or share any data.

- The app has no network permission. It cannot send anything off the device.
- It contains no analytics, advertising or crash reporting libraries.
- Diagnostic values (Android Auto host details, screen and touch information, car data you grant access to, phone state) are read while the app runs and shown on the phone and the car screen.
- Reports are saved only when you choose to, in the app's private storage on your phone. They leave the phone only when you share them yourself, with the system share sheet or the clipboard.
- Permissions are used for what their names say: car speed, fuel and mileage (CAR_SPEED, CAR_FUEL, CAR_MILEAGE) and location for the car's own sensor data, Wi-Fi and network state for the connection report. Each can be denied; the matching rows then show "permission missing".

Deleting the app deletes all saved reports.
